🌏 Blood Pressure Standards by Country 🧭

🇰🇷 KSH: Normal <120/80 · Elevated 120–129/<80 · Pre-hypertension 130–139 or 80–89 · Hypertension ≥140/90
🇯🇵 JSH 2019: Normal <120/80 · High-normal 120–129/<80 · Elevated 130–139 or 80–89 · Grade I 140–159 or 90–99 · Grade II 160–179 or 100–109 · Grade III ≥180 or ≥110
🇺🇸 ACC/AHA 2025: Normal <120/80 · Elevated 120–129/<80 · Stage 1 130–139 or 80–89 · Stage 2 ≥140/90
🇪🇺 ESC 2024: Non-elevated <120/70 · Elevated 120–139 or 70–89 · Hypertension ≥140/90
※ These are clinic-reading categories. At home, hypertension is usually defined as 135/85 or higher.

⚖️ Weight & BMI Standards by Country 🌍

🇰🇷 KSSO: Underweight <18.5 · Normal 18.5–22.9 · Overweight 23–24.9 · Obese 1: 25–29.9 · Obese 2: 30–34.9 · Obese 3: ≥35
🇯🇵 JASSO: Underweight <18.5 · Normal 18.5–24.9 · Obese 1: 25–29.9 · 2: 30–34.9 · 3: 35–39.9 · 4: ≥40
🇺🇸 CDC/NIH: Underweight <18.5 · Healthy 18.5–24.9 · Overweight 25–29.9 · Obesity 1: 30–34.9 · 2: 35–39.9 · 3: ≥40
🇪🇺 WHO (used across Europe): Underweight <18.5 · Normal 18.5–24.9 · Pre-obese 25–29.9 · Obese I: 30–34.9 · II: 35–39.9 · III: ≥40
※ "Healthy" is the weight range for your height at each standard's normal BMI. BMI can be misleading for very muscular people.
